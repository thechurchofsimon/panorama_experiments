About this draft alignment

East Gate and West Gate use Simon’s supplied coordinates, approximately 190 m apart. Vicus, Principia and the blue outlines are estimated from the marked satellite drawing using those two anchors. These are diagrammatic positions, not a surveyed archaeological plan; check the fit against paths and trees on site. The West Gate marker follows the drawing even where it sits outside the simplified outline.

North stays at the top. “My location” asks your browser for location access and shows an accuracy circle; it does not automatically open a portal. Satellite imagery and map tiles need an internet connection. Use Back to map after the panorama finishes.
